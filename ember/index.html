A practical toolkit for people who lead teams: SWOT, Important-vs-Urgent (Eisenhower), Impact-vs-Effort, a Feedback Scale, a “Bored-to-Panic” check-in, and a dashboard to pull them together. Opinionated, hands-on, made to be used in the room.

A working set

The tools I think with.

A working set of razors, laws, and rules I keep coming back to. Most aren’t mine. The ones that are, are marked.

  1. Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity.
    Hanlon’s Razor
  2. Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.
    Carl Sagan
  3. Prefer the simplest explanation that fits all the facts.
    after Occam
  4. Problems can have multiple causes.
    Hickam’s Dictum
  5. What cannot be tested is not worth debating.
    Newton’s Flaming Laser Sword
  6. The effort to refute nonsense is an order of magnitude greater than the effort to produce it.
    Brandolini’s Law✦
  7. When the facts change, I change my mind.
    Keynes
  8. Good strategy is deciding what not to do.
  9. Strong opinions, lightly held.
  10. Plain language beats jargon.
