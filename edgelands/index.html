Laws & razors

The tools I think with.

Strong opinions, lightly held. A weathered set of razors and laws I return to — out in the field and back at the desk.

  1. 01

    Never attribute to malice that which is adequately explained by stupidity.

    Hanlon’s Razor
  2. 02

    Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.

    Sagan’s Standard
  3. 03

    Prefer the simplest explanation that fits all the facts.

    Occam’s Razor
  4. 04

    Problems can have multiple causes.

    Hickam’s Dictum
  5. 05

    What cannot be tested is not worth debating.

    Newton’s Flaming Laser Sword
  6. 06

    The effort required to refute nonsense exceeds the effort needed to create it.

    Brandolini’s Law
  7. 07

    When the facts change, change your mind.

    after Keynes
  8. 08

    Good strategy is deciding what not to do.

    a working principle
  9. 09

    Strong opinions, lightly held.

    a working principle
  10. 10

    Plain language beats jargon.

    a working principle
